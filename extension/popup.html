BCL Compliance Companion

Active. Nothing to configure.
  1. Open a client in the UAE Compliance Tracker
  2. Go to Login Credentials
  3. Press Launch & fill
On EmaraTax, click “Login here” under “Non UAE PASS users may” — the fields fill as soon as that form appears.

Credentials are held for the launched tab only, for five minutes, and are never sent anywhere. This extension does not press Login.
